# ADR-001: Separate, content-addressed benchmark authority

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Decider:** Evan Smith

## Context

PhytoAllele `0.2.0a3` is an immutable research alpha whose empirical benchmark is empty. Its
statistical gates now use confidence bounds, but its row manifests do not themselves enforce
licensing, historical leakage, connected independence, external-species separation, or operational
blinding. Adding those controls to the sealed release would destroy its hashes and mix method logic
with benchmark authority.

The benchmark must also be publishable on GitHub without exposing restricted biological data or
giving the evaluated method access to truth labels.

## Decision

Create PhytoBench as a separate Apache-2.0 repository. Canonical JSON plus SHA-256 receipts are its
authority; TSV is never authoritative. It validates a source registry, candidate rows, and a freeze
contract, computes dependence components, and produces two bound outputs:

- a runner pack with opaque case IDs, pre-truth context, and salted commitments; and
- a supervisor pack with source registry, full rows, truth payloads, salts, and the initial attempt
  ledger.

Evaluation freezes fail unless effective independent denominators, source rights, source ancestry,
pre-registration, and split controls pass. Curation freezes may remain structurally valid with an
explicit empirical blocker.

## Options considered

### Patch PhytoAllele `0.2.0a3`

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Complexity | Medium |
| Auditability | Poor: invalidates a sealed predecessor |
| Separation of duties | Poor |
| Publication risk | High |

### Store benchmark rows in manuscript folders

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Complexity | Low |
| Auditability | Poor: mutable and mixed with private research artifacts |
| Blinding | None |
| Publication risk | High |

### Separate content-addressed repository

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Complexity | Medium |
| Auditability | Strong |
| Separation of duties | Strong |
| Publication risk | Contained by code/data boundary and privacy scanning |

## Consequences

- The sealed adjudicator remains unchanged and reproducible.
- Benchmark governance can evolve under its own versioning and reviews.
- Runners cannot verify or reveal truth without the supervisor pack.
- A hash proves only byte identity; independent truth, rights, and scientific reviews remain
  necessary.
- Random salts make each real blind freeze intentionally unique. Package builds remain reproducible;
  benchmark freezes are one-shot artifacts verified by receipts rather than regenerated bytes.

## Follow-up

- Add a version-pinned PhytoAllele adapter after its exact input contract is independently replayed.
- Add a hash-linked external attempt-ledger successor workflow.
- Add component-aware evaluation after benchmark outputs exist.
- Keep the public locked manifests empty until dual-curated, rights-clear rows pass every gate.
